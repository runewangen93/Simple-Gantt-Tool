• Add a block: tap or click empty space on a row
  • Move a block by dragging it, resize by dragging its ends
  • Remove a block: double-tap it
  • Reorder tasks: drag the dots on the left
  • Select several: drag a box over blocks (touch / Apple Pencil: press and hold on empty space first). Shift/Ctrl-click adds to the selection, Esc clears it, Delete removes the selected blocks. Then drag any selected block to move them together
  • Undo / redo: the arrow buttons, or Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z
  • Extras has Save data and Load data, to keep your chart as a file
